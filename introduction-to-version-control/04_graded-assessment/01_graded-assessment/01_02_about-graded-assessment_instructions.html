The Purpose of the Graded Assessment

The main purpose of this graded assessment is to evaluate your understanding of the course's key learning objectives. More importantly, it helps you identify which topics you have mastered and which ones require a bit more review before you finish the course. Ultimately, this assessment is designed to ensure you are fully prepared to succeed in the next course of this program.

How to Prepare

Throughout this course, you have completed practical exercises, knowledge checks, and in-video questions. Because this final assessment only covers material you have already learned, reviewing these past activities is the best way to study. There are no trick questions or surprise topics—if you have followed along with the lessons, you are already in a great position to succeed!

Reviewing Your Results

After you complete the assessment, please take a moment to review your results and feedback. If you miss any questions, use that feedback as a guide to go back and revisit the specific lessons that require your further attention.

Good luck!
